01 / The worldBeyond the Veil

Ancient powers have hidden in plain sight for millennia. Now their war has reached modern Los Angeles, and Onyx Esposito stands at its centre.

“Angels are not always righteous. Demons are not always wrong.”

The Celestials series follows Onyx as she embarks on an epic urban fantasy adventure alongside Raphael, a Celestial warrior, and the mysterious Chthon named Lucian.

As realms of light and shadow collide, every book peels back deeper conspiracies and forces brutal choices about loyalty, power, and what salvation really costs.

04 / The authorMeredith Taylor

Meredith Taylor is the author of the Celestials and Ridgemont University series. She enjoys a good book and characters who are pushed to the limits of love and war. She spends most of her days drifting in her own imagination.

When not writing, Meredith enjoys exotic teas and travelling. Email her at contact@meredithtaylorbooks.com.
